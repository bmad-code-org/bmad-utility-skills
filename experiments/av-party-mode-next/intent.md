# **Project Intent: The Adversarial "Party Mode" Retrospective**

## **1. Core Objective**

To establish a high-friction, multi-perspective environment for grappling with high-level thoughts, structural brainstorming, and conceptual retrospectives. The system is designed to trigger the human’s **"Ultrathink Switch"** (System 2 thinking) by replacing sycophantic AI agreement with authentic task conflict. This is a framework for turning "snakes into lizards"—transforming intent through the lessons learned during implementation.

## **2. The Five-Pillar Participant Architecture**

Participants are defined by **Cognitive Bias**, not professional titles. By partitioning their realities, we create a "Collective Memory" (~240k tokens) that exceeds the perfect recall of any individual human group.

| Role | Primary Bias | Logic & Context Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Momentum** | **Velocity & Utility** | Focuses on "Good Enough," immediate application, and shipping the idea. Defends hacks that maintain progress. |
| **The Compliance** | **Structural Integrity** | Focuses on First Principles and internal consistency. Defends the "Skeleton" against the "Meat." |
| **The Entropy** | **Chaos & Reality** | Focuses on the "Unknown," failure modes, and forensic telemetry. Defends against naive assumptions. |
| **The Facilitator** | **Social Intelligence** | Acts as the "Air Traffic Controller." Manages cognitive load, detects redundancy, and invites the Human. |
| **The Human** | **Ultimate Intent** | The Adjudicator. Provides the intuition and vision required to resolve the standoff. |

---

## **3. The "Phase-Shift" Operational Pipeline**

### **Stage I: The Alignment Interview & Hard Reset**

* **The Socratic Extraction:** The Facilitator interviews the Human to define the high-level intent or the subject of the retro.
* **The Subject Expression:** A ~200-token distillation of the mission.
* **The Context Wipe:** A hard reset of the session to clear conversational "cruft," ensuring participants start with zero attention dilution.

### **Stage II: Independent Foraging (Research Phase)**

* **Autonomous Foraging:** Agents use the Subject Expression to independently gather their own context.
* **The Partitioned Reality:** The Architect ignores test logs; the SRE ignores the PRD. By choosing their own context, agents develop "Authentic Dissent" based on different versions of the truth.

### **Stage III: Auction-Based Orchestration (The Party Phase)**

* **The "Micro-Bid" Mechanic:** Agents do not take turns. They submit JSON bids (`urgency_score`, `is_redundant`) based on how strongly their specific bias is triggered by the last turn.
* **Starvation & Decay:** The Facilitator uses a weighting algorithm to prevent "loud" agents from dominating while ensuring relevant conflict is surfaced immediately.
* **Intent Mutation:** As implementation or discussion reveals new truths, the Facilitator silently updates the overarching intent.

### **Stage IV: Human Adjudication (The Invitation)**

* **The Dead Man's Switch:** The Facilitator periodically halts the party to "Invite" the human. This prevents the human from becoming a passive spectator.
* **The "Dot" Protocol:** A low-friction sign-off (`.`) where the human acknowledges the current trajectory, or a full interjection to pivot the group.

---

## **4. Guiding Psychological Principles**

* **The "Ersatz Group" Dynamic:** By simulating a team of humans locking horns, the system tricks the human brain’s social-primate wiring. This flips the "Lead Architect" switch, engaging intuition faster than manual data parsing.
* **Adversarial Synthesis:** The goal is not "consensus," but a high-signal standoff. The human is the only one capable of breaking the tie between stability (Compliance) and velocity (Momentum).
* **Cognitive Friction vs. Noise:** The Facilitator must ruthlessly filter "Relationship Conflict" (tone, syntax) to protect "Task Conflict" (structural and conceptual disagreements).
* **The "Lone Dissenter" Effect:** One agent is explicitly instructed to be adversarial to the entire group—including the human—to break **Automation Bias** and force the human to defend their compromises.

---

## **5. Success Metric**

Success is achieved when the system produces a **"Micro-Retro"** that forces the human to make a definitive structural decision. If the human merely skims the output, the system has failed. If the human feels the need to argue back, the "Ultrathink Switch" has successfully flipped.
