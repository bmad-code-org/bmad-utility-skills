# LinkedIn Post

**When to generate LinkedIn:**
- Major version releases (e.g. v6.0.0 Beta, v7.0.0).
- Minor releases with exceptional new features.
- Community milestone announcements.

Skip LinkedIn for routine patch releases — it's for releases that carry significant news.

## Approach

- Professional, measured tone — written as the maintainer addressing a developer/leadership audience.
- Lead with a one-line statement of what released and what it's for.
- Group the 2–4 most significant changes under short bold headers, each with a sentence on the **user/team benefit** (not implementation detail).
- Reinforce the free-and-open-source positioning once, plainly.
- Close with a soft engagement prompt (invite the reader to share their own tools/patterns).

## Guidelines

- Plain text with bold sub-headers; no bullet clutter.
- ~800–1,500 characters.
- Near the end, low-key: the **`bmadcode.com` blog/site link** and the GitHub link (e.g. "More at bmadcode.com · github.com/bmad-code-org").
- End with 4–6 professional hashtags (e.g. `#AI #OpenSource #SoftwareDevelopment #DevTools #Agile #AgentEngineering`).
